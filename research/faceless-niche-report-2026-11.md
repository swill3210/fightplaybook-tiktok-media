# Faceless YouTube Niche Research — November 2026

**Data date:** 2026-11-25 · **Source:** vidIQ connector (live YouTube data) · **Credits used:** 20 of 20

## Read this first: what this report is and isn't

- **Scope was limited by credits.** The vidIQ account had 20 credits (4 calls). I used them on:
  1. A breakout-channel scan (faceless, long-form, <90 days, <50K subs). **It returned 0 results** because the filters were too narrow. Those 5 credits were wasted.
  2. An outlier scan of every English long-form video from the last 30 days on channels under 50K subs with 100K+ views. It returned 84 videos.
  3. An outlier scan focused on narrated "what life was like" history, last 3 months. It returned 39 videos.
  4. A batch lookup of 44 channels for creation date, subscribers, total views and video count.
- **You asked for 15 verified niches. The data supports 9.** Each of those 9 has at least 3 real channels behind it. Six more appeared in the data but each rests on only 1–2 channels, so under your rule (no niches from a single viral video) I list them as **unverified leads** and don't rank them.
- **Not verified:** thumbnails (I didn't view the images), exact 30-day channel views, and per-video earnings. I didn't spend credits on vidIQ's earnings tool.
- **All RPM and revenue figures are my own estimates** from typical ranges for each category. They are not vidIQ data. Revenue only starts after YouTube Partner Program approval (1K subs + 4K watch hours, or 10M Shorts views).
- **Upload frequency** = total videos ÷ weeks since the channel was created. The video count may include Shorts.
- **"Monthly views"** = lifetime views ÷ months since creation. It's an average, not true 30-day views.
