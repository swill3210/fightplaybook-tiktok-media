# Wild punch / haymaker animation instructions

Animate a realistic wild punch (haymaker) using accurate human biomechanics. The attacking character throws a powerful, uncontrolled, looping punch with a wide arm swing. This complements the fight-playbook-fighting-technique-animation skill and never overrides Fight Playbook branding or character rules. A copy lives in `/workspace/fightplaybook/standards/technique_library/wild_punch_haymaker.md`.

## Movement
- **Wind-up:** the attacker pulls their punching arm backward, exposing their chin and leaving their face unprotected.
- **Arm movement:** the fist travels in a wide horizontal or diagonal arc toward the opponent's head, rather than straight forward.
- **Body rotation:** the shoulders, hips and torso rotate aggressively in the direction of the punch.
- **Footwork:** the attacker may step forward or overcommit their weight, causing temporary imbalance.
- **Impact trajectory:** the fist swings around the outside of the opponent's guard, targeting the side of the head.
- **Missed punch:** if the opponent ducks, slips or steps away, the attacker's momentum carries their upper body slightly past the target.
- **Recovery:** the attacker must naturally regain balance and bring their punching hand back toward their face.

## Critical animation rules
- Arms keep realistic anatomical proportions and joint movement.
- Elbows bend naturally without twisting or deforming.
- Hands stay connected to the wrists and forearms.
- Punches follow smooth, continuous arcs.
- Avoid robotic movement, floating limbs, exaggerated stretching and unnatural shoulder rotation.
- Show the punch from front, side and overhead angles to clearly demonstrate its trajectory (use these as review angles even when only one makes the final cut).

## Important
A wild punch should look aggressive, wide, uncontrolled and overcommitted. It must NOT resemble a technically correct boxing hook, jab or straight punch. When zoom reviewing a still, reject it if the attacker's guard hand is tight, the arm is compact at 90 degrees, or the punch path looks straight.
