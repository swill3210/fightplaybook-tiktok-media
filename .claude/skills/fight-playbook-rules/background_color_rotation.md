# Background Color Rotation (MANDATORY as of Nov 18, 2026, from Sean)

This applies to everything: YouTube long form, Shorts, TikTok, Skool Academy lessons, and the trailer. It replaces the old pale blue only default.

- Flat solid background, rotating through: pale blue, warm sand, sage green, slate gray, deep teal, cream.
- Never pink or lime.
- Never use the same color back to back from one video to the next. Don't repeat a camera angle back to back either.
- No hex codes have been given yet. Tik Tok Clips may have exact values.

Hex codes (from Tik Tok Clips, Nov 19 2026): pale blue #D8EEFE, warm sand #ECD6B0, sage green #C4D6C4, slate gray #BEC6D0, deep teal #30767C, cream #F5ECD6. See background_color_hex.md.
