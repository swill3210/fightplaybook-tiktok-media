# Lead hook to the liver
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_jab_liver (How to Use a Jab to Set Up a Liver Shot). Source: FIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_ANIMATION.md sections 4, 9, 11 and 15; Satori Fight Club, Body hook; Box With Simmy, How to Throw a Body Shot.

**Alternative names:** liver shot, liver hook, left hook to the body (orthodox), lead body hook, rip. A slightly upward version is the shovel hook (section 9).

**Starting stance (orthodox):** left foot forward, right foot back, gloves at the cheeks, elbows on the ribs, chin tucked. Close to mid range, usually right after a head shot (a jab) has lifted the opponent's guard and elbows.

**Arm and leg positioning:**
- LEAD (left) arm punches. Elbow bent about 90 degrees (adjust for range), elbow close to the own side, fist travels with the knuckles turned in. NEVER a straight arm.
- REAR (right) glove stays tight on the right cheek and chin the whole time.
- BOTH knees bend to drop the level so the fist reaches the lower ribs. Back stays upright; never bend at the waist.

**Trajectory:** a short, compact curve from the side into the target, horizontal or angled slightly upward under the ribs. Not a wide windmill swing, not a straight line.

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** the lead hip and shoulder turn toward the target together with the punch; power comes from the legs and hips, not the arm alone.

**Foot placement and pivot:** pivot on the ball of the LEAD (left) foot, heel turning out; rear foot stays planted. Feet stay about shoulder width, no crossing.

**Target:** the opponent's RIGHT side, just under the bottom of the rib cage, under their right elbow (orthodox vs orthodox). Not the elbow, not the kidney or back (foul), not the floating ribs straight on.

**Recovery:** come straight back up into stance with the lead glove back at the cheek, or follow with a head shot as the opponent's elbow drops. Never stay low.

**Opponent reaction (15.11):** folds or twists toward their own right side, right elbow clamps down over the liver, guard breaks.

**Common animation mistakes:**
- The arm drawn nearly straight (140 to 160 degrees at the elbow): reads as a straight body punch, not a hook. This is the most common image model failure in SIDE VIEW, because a flat horizontal hook points its upper arm at the camera and foreshortens.
- Landing on the partner's chest or elbow instead of under the ribs.
- Bending at the waist instead of the knees.
- Rear hand dropping from the chin.
- Hero placed on the LEFT facing RIGHT in a side view: then the partner's right side (the liver) is on the FAR side of the partner and the punch looks like it lands on the wrong side or behind the body.

**Real catches in builds (tt_jab_liver, Nov 23 2026):**
- 9 tries rejected before the kept still: first try side view (arm about 140 degrees, read as a straight body punch), 2 side view retries with "very close, sharp L shape" wording (still about 150 to 160 degrees), first try and 2 retries of a front three quarter view (same straight arm), a high angle try (straight arm) and a high angle try where the glove disappeared behind the partner's arm toward the back. One more side view "V" try was close but the other one was better.
- What worked (lv_liver, kept): SIDE VIEW, main character on the RIGHT facing LEFT so the lead arm and the partner's liver side face the camera, and describing the arm as a V seen from the side: "the upper arm angles DOWN and forward from the shoulder so the ELBOW is LOW, close to the own hip, and the FOREARM angles UP and forward from that elbow so the knuckles dig in and slightly upward ... about 90 degrees ... the glove stays close to the own body, less than a forearm length in front of the own stomach ... NOT extended". This reads as a short bent arm hook digging up under the ribs (a shovel style liver hook, allowed by section 9).
- Second angle check (lv_liver_rear, rear three quarter from behind the lead shoulder): same moment reads as a bent arm hook wrapping around into the right side under the raised elbow. Not used in the cut because there is no clear background behind the fist for speed lines.
- Pivot on the lead foot is hard to see in the side view still (both feet planted); hip turn and knee bend are visible.

**Best camera angles:** side view with the puncher's lead side toward the camera (level change, bent arm, target under the elbow); rear three quarter from behind the lead shoulder (shoulder rotation and the wrap around into the side); a front view hides the arm.
- Mistake still (lone hook into the elbow, lv_hook): try 1 and both try 2 variations came out with the arm at 125 to 150 degrees; the same "V seen from the side" wording fixed it on try 3 (variation a kept).
- tt_liver_vs_head (Nov 23 2026): the same V wording (prompts/ws_liver.txt, plus the partner holding a tall head guard and buckling toward the right side) worked on the FIRST try: variation a kept (elbow low by the hip, forearm angled up, fist under the right ribs under the raised elbow, rear glove on the chin); variation b drew a straighter arm (about 140 degrees) landing on the belly and was rejected. Second angle check (rear three quarter from behind the lead shoulder, ws_liver_rear) passed first try: bent arm wrapping into the partner's right side under the raised elbow, rear glove up. The partner did not visibly buckle at contact in the kept still; the drop is shown in its own result still (ws_drop: partner down on the right knee, right elbow and glove clamped over the liver).
