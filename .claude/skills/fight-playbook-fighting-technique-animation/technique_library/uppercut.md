# Uppercut (short rear uppercut up the middle)
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_haymaker_counters. Sources: Boxing IQ Japan (drive the uppercut in a straight line), Honour and Glory Boxing (inside slip to lead uppercut), RDX Sports (uppercut through the gap of a haymaker).

**Alternative names:** uppercut, rear uppercut, right uppercut (orthodox), the six.

**Stance:** knees bent, close range, inside the opponent's arm.

**Arm positioning:** elbow bent about 90 degrees and FIXED, forearm vertical, palm facing your own body, elbow close to your own ribs. Other glove stays on the cheek.

**Trajectory:** straight UP by the shortest path from below the chin line to the chin; never a big scoop.

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** rear hip and shoulder turn up and forward; legs drive.

**Target:** point of the chin, from underneath.

**Recovery:** fist straight back to guard.

**Opponent reaction:** head snaps up and back.

**Common animation mistakes:**
- Drawing it as a hook (horizontal elbow) or a straight.
- Big scooping swing from the hip.
- Speed lines: the uppercut lives between two bodies, so there is no clear background beside it. tt_haymaker_counters used VERTICAL streaks (direction 2, wrapper in the build script) limited by slzones to the partner's torso left of the forearm. Sean's call whether that is acceptable.

**Best camera angles:** side or high angle side with the attacker's missed arm looping past.
