# Wild Punch / Haymaker (Sean's approved instructions, Nov 23 2026)

Animate a realistic wild punch (haymaker) using accurate human biomechanics. The attacker throws a powerful, uncontrolled, looping punch with a wide arm swing.

- Wind-up: punching arm pulls backward, exposing the chin and leaving the face unprotected.
- Arm movement: fist travels in a wide horizontal or diagonal arc toward the head, not straight forward.
- Body rotation: shoulders, hips and torso rotate aggressively in the direction of the punch.
- Footwork: attacker may step forward or overcommit weight, causing temporary imbalance.
- Impact trajectory: fist swings around the outside of the opponent's guard, targeting the side of the head.
- Missed punch: if the opponent ducks, slips or steps away, momentum carries the upper body slightly past the target.
- Recovery: attacker naturally regains balance and brings the punching hand back toward the face.

Critical rules:
- Realistic arm proportions and joint movement; elbows bend naturally without twisting or deforming.
- Hands stay connected to wrists and forearms.
- Smooth continuous arcs; no robotic movement, floating limbs, exaggerated stretching or unnatural shoulder rotation.
- Show the punch from front, side and overhead angles to demonstrate the trajectory.

Important: a wild punch must look aggressive, wide, uncontrolled and overcommitted. It must NOT resemble a technically correct boxing hook, jab or straight punch.
