# High guard (tight guard, shell, catching head shots)
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_liver_vs_head. Source: FIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_ANIMATION.md section 10 (High guard); BoxingWiki, High Guard; Become Fighter, How to block punches with a high guard; Boxing Learn, Boxing High Guard.

**Alternative names:** high guard, tight guard, earmuffs, double arm block, shell (loosely).

**Starting stance:** normal boxing stance, knees soft, weight centred.

**Arm and leg positioning:** both gloves high against the forehead, temples and cheeks; forearms roughly vertical; elbows tucked toward the ribs; chin tucked down, eyes looking through or over the gloves; neck braced. Feet planted, about shoulder width.

**Trajectory / movement:** small; the gloves meet the punch close to the head and the arms firm up for a moment, then return to normal guard. No reaching out.

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** minimal; shoulders slightly raised around the chin.

**Foot placement and pivot:** planted; can step forward behind the guard (walking through punches, as in tt_liver_vs_head).

**Target protected:** chin, jaw and temples. What it opens: lifting the elbows to cover the head opens the body, especially the liver under the right elbow (orthodox).

**Recovery:** back to the normal guard, then move or punch.

**Common animation mistakes:** gloves floating in front of the face instead of touching it; elbows flared so the guard looks like a frame; chin up; eyes hidden behind the gloves; the punch passing through the gloves to the face.

**Real catches in builds:** tt_liver_vs_head ws_head: the partner's tight guard caught the cross on the gloves first try (both variations). ws_target: asked for the right elbow "lifted up and away" for an open liver; the model drew the elbow only slightly forward, but the gap under the elbow to the lower right ribs still read clearly.

**Best camera angles:** side view (chin tuck, forearm wall, the gap under the elbow); front view (glove placement).

**Extension Nov 23 2026 (tt_protect_chin):** bladed variant used for chin protection: see chin_tuck_behind_lead_shoulder.md and catch_block_straight_right.md (lead shoulder up, chin behind it, gloves at the temple and cheek, punch caught on the glove).
