# Chin tuck behind the lead shoulder (bladed chin protection posture)
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_protect_chin. Sources: FIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_ANIMATION.md sections 1 (stance), 2 step 4 (lead shoulder rises to protect the chin), 10 (High guard, Shoulder roll); USIBA Official Manual, The Sweet Science 101 (chin pinned down, only the top of the head exposed); Total Rounds, Tuck your chin behind your lead shoulder; Become Fighter, How to throw a jab; H&G Boxing, Boxing Stance and Guard Basics; Diesel Gym and Kali Sikaran on bladed stances.

**Alternative names:** chin tuck, chin down, shoulder up, bladed guard, "hide the chin".

**Starting stance:** orthodox boxing stance, left foot forward, right foot back and slightly out, knees soft, weight centred. Turned partly sideways (about 45 degrees), lead shoulder and lead hip toward the attacker. NOT fully side on (the sources warn a fully bladed stance is weak against grabs and takedowns).

**Arm and leg positioning:** lead shoulder shrugged up in front of the lead side of the jaw; chin tucked down toward the chest behind it so only the eyes show above it; both gloves high (one at the temple, the other at the cheek beside the chin); elbows tucked against the ribs; eyes up, looking through the top of the eyes, never at the floor.

**Trajectory / movement:** small: the body turns from square to bladed, the lead shoulder rises, the chin drops. Head moves with the trunk.

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** hips and shoulders turned together about 45 degrees so the chest narrows to the attacker.

**Foot placement and pivot:** lead foot points at the attacker, rear foot back and out; ready to step off at an angle (lateral step or pivot, section 11).

**Target protected:** chin and jaw (the main knockout target), also the centreline.

**Recovery:** stays in the posture; after blocking, step off at an angle and reset the guard.

**Common animation mistakes:** chin level or up while the gloves are high (the image model's default face forward head); shoulder not raised so the jaw shows; gloves floating in front of the face; looking down at the floor; a fully sideways, crossed foot stance; drawing a square stance when "turned" is asked from a front or over the shoulder camera.

**Real catches in builds (tt_protect_chin):** first try (camera behind the main character) the chin tuck was hidden by the back of the head: the over the shoulder camera must sit behind the ATTACKER so the viewer sees the chin. v3 anchor (pc_anchor3) drew guard high but the torso almost square; v4 wording "clearly BLADED, turned about 45 degrees ... the LEFT shoulder shrugged up high in front of the chin and the chin tucked down behind it, only the eyes show above it" worked first try (pc_anchor4_a kept). In that still the lead glove sits lower in front of the chin and the rear glove at the temple: acceptable guard variant.

**Best camera angles:** front or over the attacker's shoulder (shows chin, shoulder and both gloves); side view (shows the chin tuck depth and the blade angle).
