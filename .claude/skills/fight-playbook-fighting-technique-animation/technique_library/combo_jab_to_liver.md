# Combination: jab to the head, lead hook to the liver (jab high, hook low)
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_jab_liver (How to Use a Jab to Set Up a Liver Shot). Source: FIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_ANIMATION.md sections 2, 4, 9 and 14; Box With Simmy (jab high, body shot low); Evolve Daily (jab to the top of the head to bring the guard up, then the liver shot); Fight Encyclopedia (jab high, hook low; a liver shot with no setup hits the elbow).

**Alternative names:** jab to the body hook, one to the liver, jab high hook low, high low combination, jab to shovel hook.

**Starting stance (orthodox):** left foot forward, gloves at the cheeks, chin tucked, at jab range.

**Sequence (each punch its own image, section 14):**
1. JAB: lead (left) arm straight and horizontal to the face; rear glove on the right cheek; lead shoulder up. The opponent lifts both gloves to the face and the elbows rise, opening the right side.
2. Retract the jab along the same straight line (do not let the hand jump positions; show a guard or the next punch, never a teleport).
3. LEVEL CHANGE: bend both knees (not the waist), step in slightly with the lead foot if needed.
4. LEAD HOOK TO THE LIVER: lead elbow bent about 90 degrees, hips and shoulders turn, pivot on the ball of the lead foot, fist into the opponent's right side under the ribs and under the raised right elbow; rear glove stays on the chin.
5. RECOVER: back up into stance, gloves at the cheeks (or follow with a head shot as their elbow drops).

**Arm and leg positioning:** the same hand throws both punches (lead), so the rear hand never leaves the chin. Feet never cross.

**Trajectory:** straight line at head height, then a short curve at rib height. The change of target height comes from the legs (section 14: "change target height using realistic leg and torso movement").

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** small on the jab; full lead hip and shoulder turn on the hook.

**Foot placement and pivot:** jab with feet in stance (small lead step allowed); pivot on the lead foot on the hook.

**Target:** jab = face or top of the head (to draw the guard up); hook = right side under the ribs (liver).

**Recovery position:** orthodox guard, upright, chin tucked.

**Common animation mistakes:**
- Showing only the hook (no visible setup), so the lesson is lost.
- Jab drawn as a hook, or hook drawn as a straight (see jab.md and lead_hook_to_liver.md).
- Switching hands between the two punches (rear hand jab or rear hand hook) or mirroring the stance between stills.
- Opponent's guard not reacting: the jab still must show the gloves and elbows lifting; the hook still must show the elbow still up.
- Staying low after the hook.

**Real catches in builds (tt_jab_liver, Nov 23 2026):** placing the hero on the RIGHT facing LEFT in every still keeps both punches on the camera side and the liver visible; the mistake still (same hook with no jab) shows the knuckles landing on the clamped right elbow.

**Best camera angles:** side view with the puncher's lead side toward the camera for both punches; rear three quarter for the hook's rotation.
