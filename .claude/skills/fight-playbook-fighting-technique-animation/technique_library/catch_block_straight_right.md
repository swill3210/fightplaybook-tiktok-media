# Catch / glove block against a straight right (with the chin tucked)
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_protect_chin. Sources: FIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_ANIMATION.md section 10 (Catch: receive an incoming punch against a properly positioned glove while maintaining guard; High guard); Urban Menton, How to Block a Hook Without Losing Your Balance (glove and forearm meet the punch, not the face; block first, then pivot or step out); Hayabusa, Blocking a Hook with Gabriel Varga (hands connect to the head, never leave the chin exposed); RDX Sports, How to Block a Lead Hook (step to the side to break the line).

**Alternative names:** catch, glove block, parry block, "take it on the glove".

**Starting stance:** bladed chin protection posture (chin_tuck_behind_lead_shoulder.md).

**Arm and leg positioning:** the glove on the side the punch arrives stays pressed to the own face (cheek or temple) and the punch lands on the glove, not the face; forearm close to the head; elbow down and in; the other glove stays at the chin; chin down; eyes up. No reaching out to meet the punch.

**Trajectory / movement:** the glove barely moves; it braces as the punch lands, then the defender steps off at an angle (section 11).

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** minimal; a slight shoulder turn toward the punch.

**Foot placement and pivot:** feet planted for the block, then a lateral step or pivot off the line.

**Target protected:** chin and jaw.

**Recovery:** guard stays up through the step; reset at the new angle.

**Common animation mistakes:** the punch passing through the glove into the face; the blocking glove held away from the head; elbow flared; chin up behind the glove.

**Real catches in builds (tt_protect_chin):** the image model would not draw a bent arm head HOOK from the attacker in any camera: 12 hook images (camera behind the main character x4, behind the attacker x4 with "letter L, elbow out to the side" wording, plus 2 side view checks) all came out as a straight or nearly straight arm (about 140 to 160 degrees) landing on the near side of the face. A straight arm landing on the face is a correct CROSS, so the lesson was rebuilt around a straight right (cross): mistake = straight right on the lifted chin (pc_hook4_a), fix = the same straight right caught on the raised glove (pc_block3_b). Section 3 checks pass: straight line from the right shoulder, rear heel lifted, lead glove at the attacker's own face.

**Best camera angles:** over the attacker's shoulder (shows the glove contact and the tucked chin); rear three quarter from behind the defender (second angle check, pc_block_front3: the punch lands on the outside of the glove at the side of the head).
