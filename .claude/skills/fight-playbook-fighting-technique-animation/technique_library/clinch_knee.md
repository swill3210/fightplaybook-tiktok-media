# Clinch knee (plum / double collar tie, straight knee to the stomach)
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_haymaker_counters. Sources: Self Defense Tutorials (Muay Thai clinch in a streetfight), Muay Thai Technician (countering wild punchers).

**Alternative names:** plum, Thai clinch, double collar tie, knee from the clinch, teep knee (straight knee).

**Starting position:** chest to chest; both hands clasped one over the other on the BACK of the opponent's head (not the neck front), forearms down on the collarbones, elbows tight.

**Leg positioning:** stand tall on the support foot, hips forward; the striking knee lifts with the thigh nearly level, knee sharply bent, toes pointed down, knee point driving into the stomach/solar plexus.

**Trajectory:** up and forward while the hands pull the head DOWN into the knee.

**Target:** stomach, solar plexus, ribs. Not the groin in these videos.

**Opponent reaction:** bends at the waist over the knee, posture broken, haymaker arm smothered and hanging wide.

**Common animation mistakes:**
- Partner gloves drawn with red cuffs (copied from the hero); tt_haymaker_counters hm_knee_a needed a red cuff recolored to black in post.
- Hands on the front of the neck (choke look) instead of the back of the head.
- Knee drawn hitting the thigh or groin.

**Best camera angles:** side or high angle side, knee nearest the viewer.
