# Step off at an angle (after a block)
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_protect_chin. Sources: FIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_ANIMATION.md section 11 (Lateral movement: step with the nearest foot, follow with the other; Pivot: rotate around the supporting foot); Urban Menton (block first, then pivot or step out; standing still after the block invites the next punch); RDX Sports (stepping to the side breaks the line of attack); AlphaBox Boxing Gym, The Check Hook (the pivot takes you away from the centre of the attack).

**Alternative names:** angle off, step off the line, side step, pivot out.

**Starting stance:** bladed guard right after a block.

**Arm and leg positioning:** guard stays high through the step (chin behind the lead shoulder, gloves at temple and cheek, elbows in); knees bent.

**Trajectory / movement:** step with the foot nearest the direction of travel, follow with the other foot so the stance width comes back; or pivot on the lead foot and swing the rear foot around. Never cross the feet, never step straight back in line.

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** the whole body turns to face the attacker from the new angle.

**Foot placement and pivot:** ends off the attacker's centre line, still in stance.

**Target protected:** gets the chin out of the line of the next punch.

**Recovery:** reset stance at the new angle, ready to move or punch.

**Common animation mistakes:** the defender in the same spot with only the attacker changed; feet crossed; guard dropped during the step.

**Real catches in builds (tt_protect_chin):** pc_angle3 (over the attacker's shoulder): the attacker's right hand reaches into empty air while the defender stands turned off to the side with the guard up; reads as "missed, moved", first try. The step itself is implied (one still), not shown as two positions.

**Best camera angles:** over the shoulder or high angle (shows the change of line); side view hides it.
