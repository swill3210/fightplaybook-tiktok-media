# Jab
Created Nov 23 2026 for tt_jab_liver (How to Use a Jab to Set Up a Liver Shot). Source: FIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_ANIMATION.md sections 1, 2 and 15.

**Alternative names:** lead straight, left jab (orthodox), the one, the can opener.

**Starting stance (orthodox):** left foot forward, right foot back, about shoulder width; knees slightly bent; left glove near the left cheek, right glove near the right cheek; chin tucked; elbows close to the ribs; weight balanced.

**Arm and leg positioning:** the LEAD (left) arm extends; the rear (right) glove stays against the right cheek the whole time. Legs stay in stance; a small step with the lead foot is fine, feet never cross.

**Trajectory:** a straight horizontal line from the guard to the target and back along the same line. Never a curve, never a swing.

**Hip and shoulder rotation:** minimal. The lead shoulder rises slightly to cover the chin; slight turn of the lead shoulder forward.

**Foot placement and pivot:** no big pivot. Weight stays centered; the lead foot may step forward to close range.

**Target:** the face or the top of the head / gloves (as a setup it aims at the head to make the opponent lift the guard). Head strikes land on the face or jaw, never the neck.

**Recovery:** retract quickly along the same straight path back to the left cheek.

**Common animation mistakes:**
- Drawing it as a hook or a wide swing (arm bent and curving). The jab is a straight line.
- Hyperextended or locked elbow; elbow flaring out.
- Rear hand dropping from the face.
- Throwing it with the wrong hand or mirroring the stance (rule 15.14): for an orthodox fighter the jab is ALWAYS the left hand.
- Wrist bent at an angle to the forearm.

**Real catches in builds:**
- tt_jab_liver lv_jab (Nov 23 2026): passed first try. The lead arm is fully straight and horizontal from the shoulder, rear glove at the cheek, partner's gloves lifted to catch it.

**Best camera angles:** side view (extension, distance, straight line); front view (straight path down the middle, rear hand at the face).

**Prompt wording that worked:** "the near LEFT arm is extended STRAIGHT out horizontally at shoulder height in a straight line from the shoulder, elbow nearly straight but not locked, palm facing down, the left shoulder raised to cover the chin ... This is clearly a straight punch, NOT a hook, NOT a swing. The REAR right glove stays tight against the own right cheek."
