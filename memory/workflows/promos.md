# Workflow: promos and TikTok Shop (separate)

Verbatim source: `Knowledge Base/Sources (verbatim)/Promos/promo_video_rules.md`; rules R20, R37, R46, R49.

* 20 to 30 s, 65 to 85 words; hook up to about 4 s; name a problem then straight into value; one rewatchable moment.
* May use a real illustrated room (walls, couch) with the rotation colour on the main wall; subtle synced SFX allowed.
* CTA last line: "Tap the orange cart below to grab it." Never "link in bio", "showcase" or "pinned comment". No loop; hard cut on the last word.
* Product photos are reference only; redraw the product in house style.
* Sean reviews the script first; deliver the file to Sean; never upload or hand to Tik Tok Clips unless he says so; ads changes need his go ahead.
* Approved template `uppercut_promo_v2.mp4` / `uppercut_promo_v3.md` is NOT accessible in any repo.
