# A04 Impossible joint or reversed limb (critical)
What: A joint bends the wrong way or a limb is mirrored (a right arm drawn as a left arm, thumb on the wrong side, twisted elbow, bent wrist at the glove).
Rule: Check which shoulder each arm leaves from and the thumb side; wrists straight behind the glove.
Auto screeners: none (inspector only)
