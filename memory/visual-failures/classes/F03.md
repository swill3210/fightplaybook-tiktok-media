# F03 Glove or limb buried inside the opponent or their clothing (critical)
What: The glove disappears under the opponent's shirt or into the body instead of compressing the surface; fabric drawn wrapping over the glove.
Rule: At contact the whole glove stays visible on top of the shirt; the shirt and body dent inward around it. A glove hidden under fabric fails.
